02

Hearing Aid Precautions

Hearing aids are classified as national Class II medical devices. Selection and use of hearing aids should be performed through professional hearing tests and fitting operations, and under the guidance of doctors or professional hearing aid fitters, otherwise secondary hearing damage may occur. Hearing aids can only assist hearing and cannot restore normal hearing, nor can they prevent or improve hearing damage caused by "organic lesions". After wearing hearing aids, rehabilitation training and lip-reading guidance may be needed. Please also note that if you don't use hearing aids frequently, you may not be able to get all the benefits from them. We recommend long-term wear and maintaining good wearing habits to maximize the benefits of hearing aids.

03

Hearing Aid Protection

Do not place hearing aids in humid or watery environments, and do not use any liquids to clean hearing aids. If sweat infiltrates the hearing aid, wipe it dry promptly. Never immerse hearing aids in any liquid. Remove hearing aids in advance and keep them safe when swimming, bathing, shampooing, or washing face. Wait until the ear canal is dry before putting on the hearing aid. If the hearing aid accidentally falls into water, do not dry it with a hand dryer or any dryer. Use a soft, clean cotton cloth/tissue to wipe it dry, and air-dry it naturally in a ventilated environment. If there is a malfunction, please contact the customer service center. Hearing aids should be kept away from high-temperature environments or prolonged sunlight exposure. Hearing aids are sophisticated electronic devices and should be protected from falling to the ground or severe knocks, vibrations, or impacts to avoid damage. Portable and mobile RF communication devices may affect the normal use of hearing aids. Please do not wear hearing aids during short-wave heat therapy, X-ray, MR scans, CT scans, or similar radiation treatments to avoid damage. When not using hearing aids for a long time, put them back in the charging case and try to keep the case charged to prevent the battery from being unable to be used normally due to long-term power loss.

04

Hearing Aid Cleaning

During the use of hearing aids, earwax will accumulate in the ear canal and the sound outlet of the hearing aid. Excessive accumulation of earwax will affect the effectiveness of the hearing aid. Therefore, please clean your ear canal regularly and maintain the hearing aid. Use a dry, soft cloth to clean the hearing aid. When wiping or cleaning the hearing aid, do it on a soft surface (such as placing a soft towel on the table) to avoid damage from accidental falls. Use a cleaning brush to brush downward at the sound outlet to clean earwax at the sound outlet. Remember: Never use any liquid to clean hearing aids!
